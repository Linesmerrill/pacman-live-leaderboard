Completion timer

When on, staff can enter each run’s time in seconds. The highest score still wins; the faster time breaks ties. The TV adds a TIME column.

Run timer

How long a maze session lasts before the TV finishes it automatically. One clock covers everyone in the maze at once.

Scoring

Staff tap the fruit a player brought back and how many ghosts they tagged; the score adds itself up. Switch on the fruit you actually have bean bags for, and set what each is worth. Defaults are the arcade values ÷ 10.

Ghosts tagged points for the 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th ghost — the 4th value repeats after that

Prize rounds

Every few minutes, whoever is in the top places wins candy, and the board starts fresh (it's backed up first). A round's clock starts with its first score, so quiet spells don't use one up. Kids ranked equal all win.

How to play (on the TV)

The rules run beside the leaderboard for the kids waiting in line, one at a time with a little animation of each. High scores, the countdown and FINISH still take over the whole screen.

Background music

A soft tune that plays on the TV between runs, and ducks under every sound effect. It stops while a run is under way.

Sound effects

Arcade blips on the TV: a rising jingle for each new score, a bigger one for the top 10 and the podium, and a fanfare for a new high score. The TV also has a speaker button next to + ADD PLAYER.

Blocked initials

A built-in list blocks — inappropriate combos and catches look-alike numbers (4→A, 5→S, 0→O…). Add your own below, separated by spaces or commas. Use ? as a wildcard, e.g. B?T.

Backup & export

Scores are saved on this Mac instantly. Make an extra backup copy any time, or download a spreadsheet.

Clear leaderboard

Removes every score from the TV — for example, before the next event night. A backup copy is saved automatically first.
